Why Nudge exists

Nudge started from wanting an honest, on-device answer to "how much am I actually scrolling today?" — a live count and a daily wellness score, without sending that data anywhere by default.

Open source and privacy-first

Nudge's source is published under the Apache License 2.0. It has no account, no ads, and no required trackers: your scroll and usage history lives in a local database on your phone. Anonymous analytics are opt-in and off by default, and the build linked from this site has them removed entirely. See the Privacy and permissions section on the home page, and the "Privacy & telemetry" section of the README, for the full picture.
