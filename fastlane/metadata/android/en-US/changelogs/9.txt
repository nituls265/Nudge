Fixes calibration mode staying stuck well past your actual install date on some devices — it was tied to your scroll-history staying gap-free, so a night your phone's battery manager killed background sync (Doze, OEM battery savers, phone off) could re-lock it indefinitely. Calibration now depends only on your install date, so it always finishes after 7 days no matter what. Missing history is still healed automatically in the background; it just no longer affects when your score unlocks.
