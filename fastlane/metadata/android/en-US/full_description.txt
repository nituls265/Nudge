Nudge counts how many times you scroll, across every app on your phone, and turns that into a daily wellness score meant to nudge you toward more conscious phone use — not guilt-trip you into quitting apps entirely.

<b>Features</b>

<ul>
<li>All-app scroll counter, built on Android's Accessibility framework so it works across every app, not just a hard-coded list.</li>
<li>A draggable floating bubble showing your live count on top of whatever app you're using.</li>
<li>A daily Wellness Score (0-100) broken into five drivers: Scroll Volume, Session Length, Unlock Frequency, Time Hygiene, and App Quality — so a bad day tells you why, not just that it was bad.</li>
<li>Time Hygiene further breaks down into Bedtime, Sleep Gap, and Consistency, so late-night phone use and irregular wake times are visible on their own, not buried in one number.</li>
<li>A history dashboard with 7-day, 30-day, and 3-month views, peak-activity-hour insight, and a per-app breakdown of where your scrolling actually goes.</li>
<li>Progressive interventions: a gentle nudge once you cross your own thresholds, escalating only if you keep going.</li>
<li>Pause and manual reset, for the days you don't want it tracking.</li>
<li>Optional laptop-scroll sync via a companion Chrome extension, if you want your desktop scrolling counted too.</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>

All tracking is on-device by default and stored in a local database — nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly turn on the one opt-in, anonymous analytics toggle (off by default), which sends only a random ID and the dates you opened the app, nothing about your usage itself. No Google Analytics, no advertising or device identifiers, no proprietary SDKs of any kind.

<b>Permissions</b>

<ul>
<li>Accessibility Service — to detect scroll gestures across other apps. Nudge never reads on-screen text or content, only scroll/window-change events.</li>
<li>Display over other apps — for the optional floating bubble.</li>
<li>Notifications — for reset/reminder notifications.</li>
</ul>
